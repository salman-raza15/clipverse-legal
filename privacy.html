Privacy Policy

Clip Verse

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Overview

Clip Verse is a personal content creation project using PeakCut to create and publish short videos.

2. Information Processed

The application may process video files, generated clips, TikTok account information, and authorization tokens required for publishing.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to connect an authorized TikTok account, process video content, and publish clips.

4. Data Storage

The application is operated locally for personal use. Application data may be stored on the local device.

5. Third-Party Services

Publishing through TikTok uses TikTok's APIs. TikTok handles information under its own privacy policy.

6. Data Sharing

Clip Verse does not sell personal information. Information needed for publishing may be sent to TikTok.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored application data may be deleted by the operator.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the Clip Verse operator through the project's GitHub repository.
